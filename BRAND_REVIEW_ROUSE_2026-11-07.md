## Ownability critique

- Memory test: an open r with a stepped shoulder cut; one compact silhouette, not a small product diagram.
- Rename test: the exact icon/wordmark system belongs to Rouse; it does not transfer intact to Veyra, InboxPay or Tillward.
- Category removal: the identity retains its distinctive letter opening in monochrome. Its defense is the retained opening and lettering construction, not “fast/secure/AI.”
- Substitution: a shield, chain, eye, spark, arrow or checkmark loses both the retained-counter construction and wordmark relationship.
- Familiarity risk: an r-based identity still participates in a crowded typographic category. The open lowercase shoulder/stepped terminal differs from common uppercase-R marks, including the [Rarible examples surfaced in an ecosystem directory](https://aptosnetwork.com/ecosystem/directory/rarible). This is a limited practical screen, not proof of worldwide uniqueness.
- Known same-name memory project remains acknowledged by the user's explicit choice; naming is not reopened.

Three palette territories compared: current graphite/lime/orange Trace Instrument; light oyster/cobalt Case Register; aubergine/flax Retained Knowledge. Retain Trace Instrument for continuity and the real evidence/error state roles. Primary wordmark works in black or white; lime is not required to recognize it.

Compared with prior unrelated project context: no receipt/paper-fold character, legal stamp, payment arrow or spatial-task metaphor. The icon's retained opening, dense wordmark and technical provenance rhythm carry this product; no neon gradient or stock crypto imagery.

## Kit and voice

Canonical assets: web/public/brand/rouse/. Vector and PNG mark/wordmark variants, favicon, avatar, 1200×630 social card, tokens, responsive board and usage rules.

Descriptor: Evidence-backed memory for recurring Sui transaction failures.
Existing tagline retained: The next failure shouldn't start from zero.
Voice: direct, evidence-first, precise about uncertainty.
Use: observed, simulation-tested, human-reviewed, eligible for review, completed blob.
Avoid: AI fixed it, automatically repaired, guaranteed safe, thirteen verified resolutions.

Typography roles: Space Grotesk headings; DM Sans body; DM Mono chain provenance. Kit samples self-host all three families and were checked loaded. Wordmark is custom r plus outlined Archivo Black, independent of runtime font loading. Source/license files are retained. This does not claim the deployed app's old Google Fonts import has already been migrated.

Motif: the stepped counter may appear as a sparing edge/crop interruption in brand framing. Never use the mark as an approval badge. Screenshots must retain source, reviewer, evidence tier and uncertainty; do not invent app behavior for a brand specimen.
