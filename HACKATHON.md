# HACKATHON

## Event
Total Recall — Tatum × Walrus Memory Hackathon

## Deadline and timezone
November 10, 2026 at 17:00 UTC. Build window: October 20–November 10, 2026. Results: November 17, 2026.

## Rules
Submission must be original/authorized work completed or materially advanced during the hackathon. One submission per person. Sponsors may disqualify incomplete, fraudulent, abusive, non-compliant, or unlawful submissions.

## Eligibility and team constraints
Online globally. Solo builders or teams are allowed. Entrants must be at least 18 or age of majority and not resident in a sanctioned/restricted jurisdiction. Formal rules say no more than one submission per person.

## Tracks
1. Markets & Sports Prediction — prediction apps that remember picks, outcomes, and source credibility.
2. Smart Agents for Real Life — agents that monitor inboxes, expenses, habits, or workflows and resume context across sessions/tools.
3. Consumer Finance Apps — shared budgets, spend tracking, savings goals, and payment flows; blockchain should stay unobtrusive.

## Sponsors
Walrus Foundation and Tatum Technology LLC.

## Required technologies
- Walrus Memory integrated and writing on Mainnet.
- At least 10 memories stored on Mainnet.
- Tatum RPC used for onchain data where relevant.
- Working demo and public GitHub repository.
- Published blog post covering product, Tatum + Walrus usage, and how it learned over time.

## Available technologies
- Walrus Memory SDK (`@mysten-incubation/memwal`) with remember, recall, analyze, ask, restore and AI middleware.
- Tatum Sui Mainnet RPC at `https://sui-mainnet.gateway.tatum.io`.
- Tatum Data APIs where applicable.
- Optional Tatum AI Builder at ai.tatum.io for a dedicated $500 bonus.

## Judging rubric
- Walrus and Tatum Integration — 30%.
- Technical Quality — 30%.
- Creativity — 20%.
- Presentation — 20%.
- Social sharing may earn bonus consideration.

## Prizes
- $1,000 per track: 1st $500, 2nd $300, 3rd $200.
- Best Walrus Memory — $500, for the deepest learning loop where memories visibly change the product.
- Best Tatum AI Builder App — $500.
- Third $500 special award is inconsistent across official surfaces: live event page says Best Use of Agent Payments; formal Event Rules say Mystery Bounty to be revealed on Discord. Treat as unresolved.

## Submission requirements
Submission form requests email, Discord, LinkedIn, Tatum ID, SUI address, SUI Account ID, MEMWAL_AGENT_ID, project description, 2–3 minute demo video, GitHub repo, and experience feedback.
