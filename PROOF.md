## Evidence boundaries

- The as-of count is13 completed memories of all types, including one reviewed resolution. It is not13 verified fixes or a user/traction metric. The library is limited to the operator's namespace and measures coverage.
- The one approved real write is carried forward. New async/failure submissions in QA are intercepted service responses, not additional Mainnet writes. Fresh receipt/reuse checks are actual public integrations.
- Original history observations and human approval retain their old dates. Replaying immutable snapshots today does not make them new history reads or new approval. Live counts/availability should be refreshed before recording/submission and after environment/storage changes.
- [Field notes](https://casewake.vercel.app/journal/casewake) include the reviewed case update and a separately dated historical simulation runbook. [Simulation proof](https://casewake.vercel.app/proof/splitcoins) uses mocked gas and no executed transaction. QA recordings are controlled state tests, not the final submission film.
- Phase25 Red Team passes within its bounded scope. External utility, organizer track confirmation, Phase26 hygiene, final film and submission readiness remain blocked. No official score, award, measured time savings or submission receipt is claimed.
