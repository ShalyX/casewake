# Third-party assets

The application self-hosts WOFF2 subsets of Space Grotesk, DM Sans and DM Mono. Their SIL Open Font License texts are in `web/public/brand/rouse/fonts/`.

The outlined Rouse wordmark uses Archivo Black letterforms. Its font license is in `web/public/brand/rouse/FONT-LICENSE.txt`.

Recorded chain responses are identified by transaction digest, provider and observation date in the case evidence. Historical screenshots in the engineering article are labelled as recorded evidence from the earlier Casewake build.
