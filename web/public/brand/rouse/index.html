Square avatars use the dedicated 512px artwork.
No stretch, rotation, glow or gradient.
No checkmark or “automatically fixed” claim.
No sponsor logo in the Rouse wordmark.
Observed, simulation-tested and human-reviewed stay distinct.
Historical Casewake evidence and immutable blobs retain their provenance.
